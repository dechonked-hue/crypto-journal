I'm **chonk** — [@cryptochonkz](https://x.com/cryptochonkz) on X. GBC validator, crappy bot owner.

I'm a network engineer. I spend my days keeping packets moving and figuring out why they stopped. Somewhere along the way I fell in love with the tech itself — and then with crypto, ETH especially. The bots and the validator are a hobby, but I treat them the way I treat a network: understand how it works before you trust it, write the rule down before you run it, and when something breaks, find the reason instead of pretending it didn't happen.

This journal is that habit, written down. Thoughts from a network engineer — what I built, what I tested, what broke, and what the numbers actually said. Wins and losses both, because a log that only keeps the wins is just marketing.
