ETH is $1,830. That's 62% below the peak, and it fell 27% in ten days.

So is it cheap? Yeah. Is it the bottom? Probably not.

I ran the numbers. ETH has been 60% off its peak three times before. In 2018 it fell another 61% over the next six months. In 2022, another 35%. This year it bounced a bit. Two out of three, buying the first −60% print was too early.

Widen it out: on nearly two out of three days at this depth, ETH traded at least 10% lower within six months. The median further fall was about 17%. Only one day in four was actually near the low.

But — and this is the part that matters — all three times, three years later, buyers were way up. 2018 buyers up 230%. 2022 buyers up 29%. So the question was never "is ETH cheap." It's "can you wait."

The on-chain stuff agrees it's cheap. MVRV — price versus what people actually paid on average — is under 1. That only happens at major bottoms. Realised price is around $2,100–$2,300, and we're under it.

But the money isn't coming in. ETFs bled $1.6bn in October, the worst month since they launched, and another $764m in November. BitMine, the biggest buyer, is basically done — 99% of the cap it set for itself. The Fed hiked in September and December is priced for another one.

So: cheap asset, hostile flows. That's the whole picture.

What about splitting the buy instead of going all in? I checked that too. In 2022, spreading it over three months beat buying at once by a lot — average cost 24% lower, and the only version in profit a year later. In 2018 it helped a bit. In the fast bounce this February it cost a little. Spreading wins in grinds, loses a little in Vs. And right now looks like a grind.

What does waiting actually save you? If it drops to $1,690, you get 8% more ETH for the same money. $1,500, 22% more. $1,000, 83% more. Even the worst case, the difference on a small buy is a rounding error. Not worth draining your cash over.

So: don't buy it all today. Buy some, leave orders lower, keep cash. Revisit after the Fed on 9 Dec.

The accumulation isn't the question. The timing is.

*Numbers from Binance daily closes, Aug 2017 – Nov 2026, plus on-chain and ETF flow data as of 15 Nov. Three episodes is a tiny sample — take it as history, not a forecast.*
